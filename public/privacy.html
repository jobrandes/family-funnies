Our Family Funnies

Privacy Policy

Updated November 10, 2026

Family Funnies, also called Our Family Funnies, is operated by Jo-Anne Brandes. It helps people create private family casts and fictional, affectionate comics. This policy covers family-funnies.pages.dev.

Information you provide

We process your account email and account identifier; family names and membership roles; the names, relationships, appearances, interests, habits and humor boundaries you save; reference photos; story ideas; cast approvals; and generated comics. A family manager may also save an email address on a cast member so that the person who signs in with that verified address can be offered a place in that family as a reader. That address is visible only to that family's manager, is not used to send email, and the person can decline. Only add details and photos you have permission to use. Avoid unnecessary sensitive information.

Google Photos

Google Photos is optional. When you choose it, Google asks permission to select photos using its Photos Picker. We retrieve the photos you select for that session and store copies as references for the cast member you chose. We do not receive general access to browse your entire photo library. You can also upload a file without connecting Google Photos.

Google access tokens are used temporarily in memory to retrieve the selected photos. The app does not store Google access tokens or refresh tokens in its database or browser storage. Selected photo copies remain in the family collection until removed; disconnecting Google does not itself delete those copies.

You can revoke Google access in your Google Account connections. To remove an imported reference, use the remove control beside that photo in People & pets.

Why we use this information

We use your information to sign you in, control family access, save your collection, prepare cast previews and personalized comics, handle invitations, and troubleshoot the service. Saved profiles and relevant photos are included in generation requests to OpenAI through the application's generation workflow. This is the central purpose of the service. Generated scenes and dialogue are fictional and can contain mistakes.

Who processes or can access it

Google Firebase handles account authentication. Supabase stores family records and images. Cloudflare hosts the app. OpenAI services process generation requests. These providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy policies. The operator and trusted production automation can access the information necessary to operate the service and create your comics.

Application access controls restrict family collections to their members according to their roles. Family managers can invite others. Anyone you share content with may keep their own copy. We do not sell family profiles or photos or use them for advertising. Family Funnies does not operate or train a general-purpose AI model.

Storage and deletion

Family profiles, references, cast previews and comics are stored to keep the collection available. Account sessions and necessary authentication data help keep you signed in. Hosting and service providers may retain technical logs for security and operations. We do not promise an immediate purge of provider backups or copies other people have saved.

You can edit saved profile details and remove reference photos in the app. For account deletion, removal of a family collection, or another privacy request, email jobrandes@gmail.com from your account address. We may ask you to verify ownership before acting on a request.

Children and family members

The account-creation service is intended for adults managing family collections. An adult may include a child in a family cast only with appropriate parental or guardian permission. Do not upload another person's information without the authority or permission needed to use it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The update date appears above. Contact Jo-Anne Brandes at jobrandes@gmail.com with privacy questions.
